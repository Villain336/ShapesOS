**Source:** ambientCG (https://ambientCG.com) — **all assets CC0 1.0 Universal**
(https://docs.ambientcg.com/license, verified 2026-09-27): free for
commercial use, no attribution required, may be copied/modified/redistributed.

**Why not Transparent Textures:** the site carries no license page and each
pattern is credited to an individual author with no stated rights grant —
fails the commercial-use bar, so it is not used (fail closed).
