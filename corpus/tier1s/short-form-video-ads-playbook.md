# Short-Form Video Ads Playbook

*Original synthesis of knowledge learned from TikTok Academy. Marketr-original words; factual attribution to TikTok's official curriculum.*

## The feed is the format

Short-form video advertising doesn't interrupt content — it competes with it. The user is one swipe from leaving, so every rule below serves a single metric: **keeping the thumb still.**

## Creative doctrine: made for the platform, not adapted to it

1. **Shoot vertical, native, and lo-fi.** 9:16 full-screen, shot on a phone aesthetic, edited in-app style. Repurposed TV spots and polished brand films underperform — the feed punishes anything that smells like an ad.
2. **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** Open with the payoff, the provocation, or the pattern-break: a bold claim, a satisfying visual, a question the viewer needs answered. Brand logos and slow establishing shots belong in the trash.
3. **Sound on is the default.** Design for audio — trending sounds, voiceover, music — but caption everything anyway; a large share watches muted in public.
4. **Creator-led beats brand-led.** Ads featuring real creators (not actors reading lines) in their own voice and setting consistently outperform studio creative. Give creators the brief's guardrails (claims, disclosures, brand safety) and freedom inside them.
5. **One message, one CTA.** The video sells the click; the landing page sells the product. Trying to close in 15 seconds produces 15 seconds of nothing.

## Creative testing at feed speed

- **Volume and velocity.** Winning accounts test dozens of creative variations per month: same script with different hooks, same hook with different creators, same creator with different offers. Creative fatigue arrives in weeks, not months — watch frequency and comment sentiment.
- **Spark-style boosting.** Boosting high-performing organic posts as ads (TikTok's Spark format) is the lowest-risk ad creative in existence — the market already voted. Build a pipeline: post organically, watch for winners, amplify the winners.
- **Comment mining.** The comment section is free creative research. Objections, questions, and jokes in comments become the next round of hooks and FAQ-style response videos.

## Targeting and structure

- **Broad targeting with strong creative** is the default working setup — the recommendation engine is the targeting. Layer interest/behavior targeting only for niche offers or when broad exhausts.
- **Separate prospecting from retargeting.** Video viewers (25%, 50%, 75%, 100% thresholds), profile visitors, and website visitors each get tailored creative: prospecting sells the problem and the promise; retargeting sells proof and urgency.
- **Budget for learning.** Give each ad group enough daily budget to exit learning (roughly 50 optimization events per week is the classic threshold) — starving ad groups of budget produces inconclusive tests that get misread as creative failures.

## TikTok Shop and commerce features

Where available, shoppable video collapses the funnel: discovery, consideration, and checkout happen without leaving the app. Operational rules:

- **Product cards and LIVE shopping** need their own creative rhythm — LIVE is a scheduled event (promote it like one), shoppable video is always-on inventory.
- **Fulfillment is part of the ad.** Late shipping and stock-outs surface instantly in comments and tank the account's creative performance. Don't scale Shop ads on shaky operations.
- **Affiliate creators** (commission-based) can scale volume without scaling your creative team — but vet creators, set claim guardrails in writing, and monitor what they publish under your product's name.

## Measurement and compliance

1. **Events API + pixel**, same doctrine as every platform: server-side events recover signal that browser tracking loses.
2. **Attribution windows:** test 1-day and 7-day click side by side before deciding which number you run the business on — short-form video drives heavy view-through and assisted conversion that last-click hides.
3. **Disclosures are non-negotiable.** Paid partnerships, gifted products, and affiliate relationships must be disclosed clearly in the creative itself — platform policy and FTC rules both apply, and "the creator forgot" is not a defense.
4. **Claim substantiation.** Before/after content, income claims, and health claims are the fastest path to ad rejection or account restriction. If you can't substantiate it on the landing page, don't say it in the video.
