## Originality and compliance notes

1. **No source text reproduced.** Every playbook is original expression — ideas and standard practices restated in Marketr's own words. The one high-risk source, Butterick's *Practical Typography* (free to read, all rights reserved), was handled paraphrase-only: reasoning behind the rules, no numerical prescriptions or distinctive phrasing.
2. **No verbatim quotations** from any source anywhere in the set.
3. **No URLs** in any file (verified by scan) — attribution is by source name only, so no links can drift or misattribute.
4. **Source statistics treated as study-bound.** Where sources publish benchmark numbers (Baymard checkout percentages, platform cost averages), the playbooks teach the diagnostic method and explicitly warn against treating the numbers as universal constants.
5. **Psychology rules treated as heuristics.** The Laws of UX and Growth.Design playbooks carry explicit cautions about varying scientific strength and an ethics line against dark patterns.
6. **ToS respected.** No bulk fetching, scraping, or access-control bypass was used. Research was human-scale (search-result summaries and the authors' public descriptions), and synthesis was written from those learnings.
7. **No Tier 3 content.** No paid books or courses were ingested, quoted, or paraphrased. The Directive's Tier 3 boundary is intact.
