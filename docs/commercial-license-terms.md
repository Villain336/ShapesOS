**Status: draft.** These terms are a placeholder awaiting the open-core split
decision (options A–D, staged neutrally in
[docs/open-core-options.md](./open-core-options.md) with no recommendation).
**Nothing in this document is in force.** Do not rely on it; do not buy, sell,
or redistribute under it.

## What the commercial license will cover (shape, not substance)

When the split is decided, the commercial license is expected to cover:

1. **White-label / agency resale rights** — run Marketr for clients under your
   own brand without AGPL copyleft obligations on the deployment.
2. **Hosted SaaS** — managed instances with data (SERP, LLM) bundled in.
3. **SLA & priority support** — response-time commitments and a private support
   channel (distinct from the OSS community channels in `SECURITY.md`).
4. **Enterprise pack** (if option B or C is chosen) — SSO/SAML hardening,
   audit-log retention, dedicated tenancy.

## What it will NOT do

- It will not change the license of the open core retroactively.
- It will not weaken the AGPLv3 on code already released under it.
- CLA-vs-DCO (staged as must-decide-before-public in `CONTRIBUTING.md`) must be
  resolved before any dual-licensing takes effect, since re-licensing requires
  clean contributor rights.
