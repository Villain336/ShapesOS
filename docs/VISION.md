# Marketr — The Agentic Marketing Ecosystem

*Vision document. Press/deck-ready. Private — do not publish without approval.*

## The problem

Marketing is fragmented. A typical local business juggles ten tools, a $3,000/month
agency retainer, and a stack of reports that never answer the only question that
matters: **did this make me money?** Agencies sell activity. Tools sell dashboards.
Nobody owns the outcome.

## The vision

**Marketr is the complete agentic marketing ecosystem** — not another tool in the
stack, not a reporting layer on top of one. A full marketing department as
software: it plans, creates, buys, executes, and proves — across every channel
that matters, digital and physical alike.

One platform. Every lever. Revenue proven, not promised.

## The ecosystem

**1. Digital dominance**
SEO, AI-search visibility (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and whatever answers engines
come next), content engine, paid ads — planned and executed by agents, not
assembled from five subscriptions.

**2. Physical marketing**
Billboards and OOH (programmatic buying + concierge), automated direct mail,
yard signs, door hangers, vehicle wraps. The channels local businesses actually
buy — brought inside the platform instead of left to reps and vibes.

**3. Media buying**
Programmatic DOOH, radio, newspaper, magazine, commercials (TV/CTV) — planned,
bought, and attributed in-platform. Marketr becomes the media buyer, not just
the planner. Every buy is proposed by agents, approved by humans, executed with
permission.

**4. Outreach & authority**
Guest-post outreach, digital PR, review generation, local listings and citation
management. Authority built systematically, not hoped for.

**5. AI relevance agents**
Agents whose job is boosting client relevance *in the places that matter*:
answer engines, maps, review sites, directories, category rankings. Wherever a
customer asks — human or AI — the client is the answer.

## The connective tissue

Four systems turn channels into an ecosystem:

- **Lead CRM** — the central object. Every channel feeds one pipeline: capture,
  score, nurture, convert. Without it, channels are noise.
- **Attribution** — every dollar traced to revenue. CPL, CAC, ROAS across digital
  *and* physical. The end of "we think the billboard worked."
- **Trust-ladder automation** — supervised → scheduled → autopilot. Agents act
  freely where mistakes are cheap and reversible; humans approve where money
  moves or reputation is on the line. Media spend is always supervised. Autopilot
  never buys.
- **Metering** — every action metered, every pass-through cost carried with
  margin. The business model is the architecture: usage grows with client
  revenue, not against it.

## The flywheel

**Plan → Create → Buy → Execute → Attribute → Optimize** — compounding.

Each cycle makes the next one smarter: attribution data feeds planning, winning
creative propagates across channels, budgets shift to what converts. The system
gets better the longer a client runs on it. That compounding is the moat — no
tool stack and no retainer agency compounds like this.

## Positioning

- "Your entire marketing department, as software."
- "We don't report on marketing. We do marketing — and prove the revenue."
- From replacing a $400/month tool stack, to replacing a $3,000/month retainer,
  to running the full-funnel growth engine no agency can staff.

## The end state

A business owner or an agency opens Marketr, and every growth lever — digital,
physical, media, outreach — is planned, executed, and measured by agents, with
humans approving what matters. Fewer tools. No retainer. Revenue proven.
